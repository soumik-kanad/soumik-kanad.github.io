The Nicest Place On The Internet

Back in 2016, there came a time when I was feeling down and didn't know how to get out of it. I searched the internet and found a website called The Nicest Place on the Internet. But during the pandemic, the website was gone. Today in 2025, I felt for the need to create a similar experience. This is my attempt to recreate that experience. I hope you find it as comforting as I did. Hugs to you!
